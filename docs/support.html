Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a round?

Tap the "+" button in the center of the bottom tab bar. Search for your course, enter your score and rating, add photos if you'd like, and tag your playing partners.

How does the rating system work?

Rate each course from 1-10 based on your overall experience. Your ratings help other golfers discover great courses. You can see how your rating compares to the community average on each course page.

Can I edit a round after posting it?

Yes! Go to the round you want to edit, tap the menu icon, and select "Edit Round." You can update the score, rating, photos, notes, and partners.

How do I find and follow other golfers?

Go to your Profile tab and tap "Find Friends." You can search by name or username. You'll also discover golfers through trending rounds and course pages.

How do I change my notification settings?

Go to Profile > Settings > Notifications. You can control which notifications you receive for follows, likes, comments, and tags.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This will permanently remove your profile, rounds, and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear from you! Email us at hello@linxgolf.app with details. Screenshots are always helpful for bugs.

Account Issues

If you're having trouble logging in, try these steps:

  • Make sure you're using the correct email address
  • Use "Forgot Password" to reset your password
  • If you signed up with Apple, make sure to use "Sign in with Apple"
  • Check your internet connection

Still stuck? Email us and we'll help you get back in.

Report Content

If you see content that violates our guidelines, you can report it directly in the app by tapping the menu icon on any round or profile. You can also email us at hello@linxgolf.app with details.
